# Handoff

Living project context, not a changelog: replaced in full on every patch, as if a senior
engineer were handing the work to the next one. It must always answer: where we are, what
is complete, what remains, what is fragile, what to avoid, decisions made, real technical
debt, next priorities, open risks. **Target 150 lines, hard ceiling 200** — past that it
stops being a handoff and becomes a second session log.

**Sinapsi writes this file — you do not.** It is the `handoff` field of the closing tool of
a patch, and the next close overwrites whatever is here.
